JunctionX Lisbon 2025

Past event details, references, and updates.

Event Overview

October 18-19, 2025

Instituto Superior Tecnico, Lisbon, Portugal

JunctionX Lisbon 2025 was the first edition of JunctionX in Portugal. Held at Instituto Superior Tecnico, this two-day hackathon brought together participants around the themes of artificial intelligence, finance, and society.

What Happened

The hackathon gathered students and professionals in teams to build solutions and present outcomes during the event.
